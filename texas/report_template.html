What This Registration Covers
Sales registration, not an installation license
Texas's Residential Solar Retailer registration (created by SB 1036) governs the sales/lease transaction for a residential solar system — required contract disclosures, the right to cancel, and a ban on misleading claims. It is not a license verifying installation competency. The actual installation work is separately governed by licensed Electrical Contractors under Texas's Electrical Safety and Licensing Act — and electrical contractors are exempt from registering as solar retailers at all. Verifying who is licensed to physically install this system is a separate check this report does not yet include.
